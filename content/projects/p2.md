### Abstract
This project evaluates 54 cross-sectional equity trading signals across seven factor families to determine whether multiple testing corrections, factor residualization, and signal blending improve out-of-sample portfolio stability over selecting top historical winners. Tested on a locked out-of-sample period with linear transaction costs, it demonstrates how backtest overfitting and signal collinearity erode unadjusted alpha, emphasizing the need for rigorous statistical screening in quantitative strategy construction.
