### Abstract
This project evaluates four portfolio optimization methods—ranging from classical mean-variance to Wasserstein robust CVaR—against an equal-weighted benchmark using a rolling 60-month out-of-sample backtest across 12 US industry portfolios. It investigates whether progressively mitigating parameter estimation noise and tail risk translates into genuine out-of-sample performance gains after accounting for trading costs.
